Privacy Policy

Room Area Sheet · DIY Apps Studio · Android version 1.5

Worksheets and settings

The app uses the property and room names you enter, room types and dimensions to calculate and organize your measurements. It stores that information with your selected worksheet, measurement units and app settings in the app’s private storage on your device. You can use the calculator without an account or an internet connection. The app does not upload your worksheets to DIY Apps Studio. If Android backup or device transfer is enabled, Android may include worksheet data in a backup or transfer under your system settings.

Sharing a worksheet

When you choose Share, the app prepares the selected worksheet as text or images and opens Android’s share menu. You decide which app and recipient receive the property and room names, dimensions and areas. The recipient or selected service handles the copy under its own privacy practices. The app keeps its temporary image copies in its private cache. When you generate another image, it removes old export folders more than 24 hours old.

App configuration and Google Play

When online, Room Area Sheet uses an encrypted HTTPS connection to download app configuration. The request identifies the app and may include a cached configuration identifier. The service receives ordinary connection information such as the IP address needed to handle the request. This request does not contain the worksheets or measurements you enter. The app may ask Google Play to show its rating card after you save a useful result. If you choose to rate the app, Google Play handles the rating and its associated data under Google’s Privacy Policy.

This version has no app account, active advertising placements or app analytics events. If the app’s data practices change, we will update this policy and the relevant disclosures.

Retention and removal

Worksheets remain in your app storage until you remove them or clear the app’s storage in Android settings. Clearing the app’s storage removes its local settings and files as well. Android manages any system backups. Clearing local storage does not delete exported copies already saved or sent elsewhere.

If you email support, we use your email address and the information you choose to send to handle your request. Contact us for current details about service or support records and to make a privacy request.

Contact

For questions about this policy or a request about your data, contact DIY Apps Studio at support.roomareasheet@diyappsstudio.com.
