DIY Apps Studio

DIY Play Reports

A private tool that collects Google Play reports for business analysis and app improvement.

What it does

Authorised studio operators connect a Google account that can read the studio's Play report files. The tool downloads reports for configured apps and turns them into reporting tables in Google Cloud.

Reports may include installs and uninstalls, ratings and reviews, store visitors and acquisitions, crashes and ANRs, and sales or earnings when those exports are available.

Who uses it

This tool is for authorised DIY Apps Studio operators. It is not a public sign-up service. This page provides information about the tool; it does not connect your account.

Google account permission

The tool requests read-only access to Google Cloud Storage. Google's permission can cover storage data that the connected account is allowed to read. The tool is configured to download the studio's selected Play reports. It does not request permission to change or delete source files.

Access can be removed from the connected account's Google account connections. Removing access stops future downloads after existing access tokens expire. It does not automatically delete previously imported reports.
