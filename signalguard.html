Privacy Policy

Updated 12 October 2026 · Signal Guard 1.0 · Android

Signal Guard is published by DIY Apps Studio (NAURAL PROJECT LTD). This policy explains local radio inspection, optional monitoring, stored observations and deliberate exports. Privacy contact: diyappstudio@gmail.com.

Information used by the app

Signal Guard reads Bluetooth advertisements and Wi-Fi access-point observations exposed by Android when you start inspection. Observations can include broadcast addresses, advertised names or network names, signal strength, timestamps and fields used to explain possible signal patterns. These may reveal information about nearby devices or places. The app does not pair with devices, join networks, identify owners or establish whether recording or surveillance is occurring.

Android may require location permission and enabled location services for radio discovery. Permission for discovery does not enable movement mode. Movement observation requires a separate, explicit choice for the current monitoring session and eligible foreground location access. It uses precise location fixes temporarily to check whether the same Bluetooth address was observed while the phone moved. Fixes remain in a rolling ten-minute memory buffer and are cleared when movement mode or monitoring ends. Coordinates and routes are never saved or exported by version 1.0. A saved movement result contains only the qualified observation summary, span and displacement.

Local storage and retention

Saved sessions hold radio observations, evidence-rule references, timestamps, signal-strength summaries and any repeated/movement result. Optional remembered, watched or ignored statuses and user labels persist locally. Observation and status payloads use AES-GCM encryption with a per-install Android Keystore key; indexed session metadata and ordinary preferences remain in protected app storage. Cloud and device backup of app data is disabled. This is encryption at rest, not a claim that a compromised or unlocked phone cannot expose data.

Storage is bounded to 100 sessions or 100 MiB, with at most 2,000 source identities per session. Reaching a limit pauses recording and shows the limitation; the app does not silently delete older sessions. You can delete a session, delete saved history or separately clear local signal statuses and labels. Uninstalling removes app-managed local data; loss of the installation key may make encrypted data unreadable. No recovery service is provided.

Export and sharing

The app sends no radio observations or location data to an app server. It has no account, advertising, analytics, billing or remote lookup SDK in version 1.0. Core inspection requires no internet connection.

You may deliberately save or share a session file through Android's selected destination or app. Default JSON/CSV exports omit radio addresses, advertised/network names, your labels and coordinates. A separate warned opt-in includes radio identifiers and names; coordinates remain excluded. Export summaries and timestamps can still disclose context, so review the content and destination. A destination may be a cloud provider and apply its own privacy practices. The app cannot recall exported copies or verify that a recipient read or retained them. Temporary files prepared for sharing become eligible for cleanup after 24 hours and are removed when the app next starts; deletion of local history does not delete external copies.

Controls and permissions

Inspection and monitoring start through your actions. An ordinary inspection pauses when the activity is in the background. Explicit monitoring uses Android's visible foreground-service behavior and offers an End action. It does not restart after reboot, force-stop or process death. Notification access enables observation alerts; denial does not block ordinary inspection. Only explicitly watched Bluetooth addresses may generate observation alerts. Address rotation breaks that association.

You can revoke permissions in Android Settings, end a session, turn movement mode off, mask identifiers, change appearance, delete history and clear statuses. These controls do not change any outside export. Help, privacy information and open-source notices are bundled for offline reading.

Platform services and support

Android and Google Play independently process information for their platform, permissions, installation and security functions under their own policies. If you choose to contact support, the email service you choose processes that message. Do not include nearby-device identifiers, locations or exports unless you deliberately intend to share them.

Changes and questions

We will update this policy when app data practices change. Contact diyappstudio@gmail.com with questions. There is no Signal Guard account or developer-held app-session database to delete. You can delete local history and statuses in the app, or remove all app-private data through Android settings. Copies you previously exported are controlled by their recipients.
