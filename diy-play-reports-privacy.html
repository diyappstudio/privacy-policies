Privacy Policy

DIY Play Reports
Effective date: 10 October 2026

This policy explains how DIY Apps Studio ("we", "us" or "our") handles Google account authorisation and report data through DIY Play Reports. The tool is for authorised studio operators and supports reporting for configured Google Play apps.

1. Permission and access

The tool requests the Google Cloud Storage read-only permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/devstorage.read_only. This permission can allow access to Cloud Storage data that the connected Google account is allowed to read. The tool's configuration limits its downloads to the studio's selected Play reports.

This permission does not let the tool change or delete source files. The tool does not request Gmail or Google Drive permissions, and we do not receive the operator's Google account password. Google handles sign-in and the permission screen.

2. Information processed

We store the renewable authorisation needed for scheduled downloads. Report data may include app and version identifiers, dates, installs and uninstalls, ratings, review text and replies, store visits and acquisitions, crash and ANR counts, and country, language or device breakdowns.

When sales or earnings exports are available, reports may also contain order or transaction references, product details, prices, taxes, refunds, currency and buyer region or postal information supplied by Google Play. Review text may contain information supplied by a reviewer. Not every report type is available for every app.

We also record operational information such as source file identifiers, import times, report row counts, run status and error details. Raw imported records may be retained alongside the reporting tables.

3. How we use information

We use the data to produce studio reports, compare app results, investigate reporting problems, and improve app quality and business operations. We do not sell this data or use it to serve personalised advertising. This report reader does not use Google report data to train general-purpose AI or machine-learning models.

DIY Play Reports' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements where applicable.

4. Storage, access and service providers

Authorisation is stored in Google Cloud Secret Manager. Imported records and reporting tables are stored in Google BigQuery. Authorisation values are not included in the public website or source repository.

Google provides the source reports and the cloud storage and processing services. dbt Cloud runs reporting transformations and handles their connection and execution information. Authorised studio operators and service identities can access data as needed for reporting and maintenance. Access is controlled through Google Cloud permissions. These providers may process data outside the operator's country.

5. Retention and deletion

Imported reports are kept for historical analysis and operational troubleshooting. The report reader does not currently apply a fixed automatic deletion period to all imported reports. Google Play's source retention does not automatically remove copies already imported into our warehouse.

You can request removal of stored authorisation or imported data by emailing diyappstudio@gmail.com. Include enough information to identify the account or reports, but do not send passwords or authorisation tokens. We review requests, confirm the requester's authority, and identify any records that must be retained for legal or operational reasons.

6. Removing Google account access

An operator can remove DIY Play Reports from their Google account connections. Future downloads stop after existing access tokens expire. Previously imported records remain stored unless separately deleted. Removing access to this reader does not remove other Google services or permissions.

7. Security

We use encrypted network connections and Google Cloud access controls to protect authorisation and report data. We limit access to the identities needed to operate the service. No system can guarantee complete security.

8. Website and policy changes

These information pages do not contain an account connection form or analytics scripts. The website host, GitHub Pages, may process connection information under GitHub's privacy statement. We may update this policy as the tool or its data handling changes. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date.
