DIY Apps Studio

DIY AdMob Reports

A private reporting tool for authorised DIY Apps Studio operators. It collects aggregate AdMob reports for business analysis and app improvement.

What it does

An authorised operator connects a Google account with access to the studio's AdMob publisher account. The tool downloads reports for configured apps and prepares reporting tables in Google Cloud. The initial configuration collects Age Calculator on Android only.

Reports include estimated earnings, ad requests, matched requests, impressions, clicks, and breakdowns by date, app, ad unit, ad source, format, country and app version where available.

Google account permission

The tool requests AdMob read-only permission. Google's permission covers the connected publisher account. The tool filters its report requests to configured apps. It cannot change ads, payment settings or source data.

Who uses it

This tool is for authorised studio operators. This website provides information and does not offer public sign-up or connect an account.
