Privacy Policy

XAPK Installer · Last updated: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DIY Apps Studio (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles data when you use XAPK Installer (“the app”) to select files, install apps, and view advertising. No account is required to use the app.

Files and installation

The app reads the files and folders you select. Package files, signing information, and file hashes are processed locally in private staging storage, then APKs are passed to Android for installation. Required OBB files are copied to the app’s OBB folder and verified. Replacing existing data requires your approval and uses temporary backups.

We do not upload package contents, filenames, or installation history to a DIY Apps Studio server or to an advertising service. The app does not delete your original source files. Android and your chosen file provider may use their own online services under their respective privacy policies.

Local records and deletion

The app keeps the last 50 completed operation records and up to 300 diagnostic events on your device. These records may include filenames, package details, times, and results. Unfinished recovery records and required working files are retained until the relevant operation is resolved.

Completed working files and replacement backups are cleaned up after verification. Clearing the app’s history does not remove an unfinished recovery, all diagnostics, or a current operation. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes its private recovery data and preferences, but does not remove source files, installed apps, or published OBB files.

Google advertising services

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertising and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage consent where required. Google may collect and share your IP address (including an approximate location derived from it), device and advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention.

UMP contacts Google to determine applicable privacy requirements and to store your choices. Ad requests begin only when UMP permits them. Google transmits this data using TLS. Installing local files does not require an advertisement to be loaded. For more information, see the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

Where required, advertising privacy choices are available from Help in the app. Android settings may allow you to reset or delete your advertising ID and revoke file or installation permissions. Changing a preference does not delete data that Google has already received; use Google’s privacy controls for such requests.

Security and third parties

Package checks verify structure and consistency; they do not guarantee that a package is free from malware. Install only files that you trust. Android, document providers, and Google services handle data under their own privacy policies.

The app does not request access to contacts, microphone, camera, or precise location. It does not use custom remote analytics or a DIY Apps Studio account database.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app’s data practices change. Please check this page and its update date for changes.
