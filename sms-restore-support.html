SMS Restore Support

Published by DIY Apps Studio, NAURAL PROJECT LTD. Email info@nauralproject.com for help or privacy questions.

Restoration needs a backup containing the SMS you want. SMS Restore cannot recover deleted messages without a copy.

Start a backup

  1. Choose Backup SMS and allow SMS access.
  2. Choose all accessible SMS or selected conversations and a backup folder.
  3. Set and confirm a password for the first backup. Keep it safely: the publisher cannot reset it.
  4. Create the backup. The app reopens the saved file and checks its contents before showing it as verified.

Keep an encrypted copy outside your phone to protect against loss of the device. Your selected document provider controls any upload to its own storage service.

Restore selected SMS

  1. Choose an SMS Restore backup, or a supported uncompressed SMS Backup & Restore XML file.
  2. Unlock an encrypted copy if asked. Preview and select the messages you need.
  3. Review how many selected SMS are already on the phone and how many are missing.
  4. Allow the temporary default SMS role. The app saves a safety copy, adds missing SMS and checks added records.
  5. Check the result. If restoration is paused or uncertain, keep the source and resume the existing operation.
  6. Use Return to my SMS app and select your usual messaging app in Android settings. Resolve pending incoming MMS first if the app asks.

While default, SMS Restore handles incoming SMS/MMS and lets you send a new SMS or reply. Restored old messages are never sent. Mobile carrier charges and delivery conditions may apply. MMS archive restoration, RCS, WhatsApp and call logs are outside the supported backup format.

Automatic backups

First create a verified backup and select a folder with ongoing access. In Settings, choose Daily or Weekly. The app reads SMS and saves encrypted copies even when closed; Android may delay work. If a destination, read permission or key becomes unavailable, repair the reported problem before expecting new backups. Turn scheduling off in Settings whenever you want.

Delete local data

Use Erase staged source in Settings to remove the imported working copy when no unfinished restore needs it. Delete a destination backup from that backup's details or through its file provider. Deleting a backup does not delete SMS on your phone. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes private data and device-bound keys, but leaves Android's messages and destination backups. Preserve your portable files and password first.

The app has no account. To remove phone SMS, use your usual messaging app. The publisher has no remote message database and cannot remove recipient, carrier or external-storage copies. For information you voluntarily sent to support, contact info@nauralproject.com.

Send a useful support request

Describe the step that failed, your phone model, Android version and SMS Restore version. You can export the content-free diagnostic report from Settings and choose whether to attach it. Do not include message text, phone numbers, passwords, real backup files or screenshots containing private messages.
