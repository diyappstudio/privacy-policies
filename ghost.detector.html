Ghost Detector Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DIY Apps Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use the Ghost Detector Android application (“Ghost Detector” or the “App”). Ghost Detector is an entertainment experience and does not provide scientific or paranormal detection.

1. Information stored by the App

Ghost Detector does not require an account. Scan sessions, encountered entities, conversation history, archive records, preferences, and generated images are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives this locally stored content.

2. Advertising and third-party data

The App may display interstitial advertising provided by Google AdMob at natural breaks after a completed session. Ads are not placed over the scanner, questions, conversations, or Archive content.

Google Mobile Ads and its advertising partners may automatically collect or receive information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it;
  • device, app, and advertising-related identifiers where permitted;
  • advertising views, clicks, and other interactions;
  • device and app diagnostics, performance, and fraud-prevention signals.

This information may be used to provide, measure, limit, secure, and personalise advertising, subject to the user’s choices and applicable law. Ghost Detector does not request access to your device’s precise location.

For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising.

3. Consent and privacy choices

Where required, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to request and record advertising consent before requesting eligible ads. The message provides choices to consent, not consent, or manage options.

You can revisit or withdraw eligible advertising choices through the “Privacy and cookie settings” entry in the App’s Settings screen. Depending on your choice and region, the App may show non-personalised or limited ads, or may be unable to request an ad.

4. Sharing and wallpapers

When you choose Share or set a wallpaper, the App creates an image in private local storage and opens the relevant Android system interface. You choose the destination. Any selected third-party app or Android component handles the file under its own privacy terms.

5. Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device until you delete them using available App controls, clear the App’s storage, or uninstall the App. Generated files may remain in destinations you selected. Advertising and consent data handled by Google is retained according to Google’s policies and your privacy choices.

6. Security

We limit developer-controlled storage to local App data and use platform security features and encrypted network transport provided by Android and the integrated services. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Children

Ghost Detector is intended for users aged 16 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

8. International processing

Google and its partners may process advertising and consent information in countries other than your own, subject to their safeguards and applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its services, or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact DIY Apps Studio at diyappstudio@gmail.com.
