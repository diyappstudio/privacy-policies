Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 26, 2026

Age Calculator ("App") is operated by DIY App Studio ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We are committed to protecting your privacy. The App is designed to function with minimal data collection:

1.1 Information You Provide

The App allows you to voluntarily enter the following information, which is stored locally on your device:

  • Date of birth
  • Name (optional)
  • Preferred language
  • App settings and preferences

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

We do not automatically collect any information when you use the App. We do not collect device identifiers, usage data, location data, or any other information automatically.

2. How We Use Your Information

All data you enter is processed locally on your device to provide the App's core functionality (age calculations, countdowns, and life statistics). We do not access, transmit, or store your data on any external servers.

3. Data Storage and Security

All user data is stored locally on your device using device-native storage. The data remains on your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party. You may delete all stored data at any time by clearing the App's data through your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share your personal information with third parties. Since all data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted, there is no data available for third-party access.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information promptly. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us.

6. Data Retention

Since all data is stored locally on your device and not on our servers, we do not retain any of your personal data. You may delete all stored data at any time by clearing the App's data through your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective date" at the top of this policy. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access — You may request a copy of the data we hold about you (which, in our case, is no data as it is stored locally on your device only).
  • Right to rectification — You may request that we correct any inaccurate information.
  • Right to erasure — You may request that we delete your personal data. Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time through your device settings or by uninstalling the App.
  • Right to data portability — You may request a copy of your data in a commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — You may object to our processing of your personal data.

These rights are provided in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and other applicable data protection laws.

9. International Data Transfers

Since all data is stored locally on your device and we do not transmit or process any user data on external servers, no international data transfers occur through the use of this App.

10. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not use cookies, pixels, or similar tracking technologies.

11. Device Permissions

The App requests the following Android device permissions to provide its core functionality:

  • Internet — Required for initial app content delivery. No personal data is transmitted over the network by the App itself.
  • Vibrate — Used to provide haptic feedback when you interact with the App's interface.
