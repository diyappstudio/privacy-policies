3. Information We Collect

Age Calculator is a utility app designed to calculate age based on dates you enter directly into the app.

  • Profile and preferences: Your optional name, selected avatar, birth date/time, comparison date and settings are saved locally on your device so the App can restore your profile and calculate life statistics. We do not send this profile to our own servers or deliberately include it in analytics or ad requests. Android backup and restore may include this local data depending on your device settings. If you choose to share a card or text, its content is sent to the app or recipient you select.
  • Device and usage data: Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics collect usage events, device/app information, identifiers and crash/diagnostic information to measure usage and stability. Automatic events may include purchases and ad interactions where supported by these services.
  • Advertising: Where banners are available, Google AdMob may process Advertising ID and other device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app/ad interactions and diagnostic data. These services process data under Google's privacy policy and applicable consent settings.
  • Country eligibility: To limit banners to Israeli mobile networks, the App checks the current mobile network's country code locally. Unknown countries do not enable banners. This check does not use GPS or an external IP-country lookup; advertising services may separately infer approximate location from network data.
  • Subscriptions: Google Play handles subscription checkout and payments. The App receives product identifiers, purchase tokens and purchase/subscription status from Google Play to acknowledge payments and restore ownership. Our billing integration does not log purchase tokens or send them to a separate developer-operated backend. We do not receive your card details; Google may provide order and financial information through Play Console.

The App does not require an account or ask for your email address or phone number. An optional profile name remains local as described above. Google Play may process account and payment information when you make a purchase.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Your profile and preferences are stored locally until you edit them, clear the App's data or uninstall the App; Android backup/restore may retain or restore them according to your device settings. We do not operate a profile or subscription-token backend. Google retains purchase/order records under its own policies. Technical data and consent records processed by third-party SDKs are retained under their providers' retention policies.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed by Google services for analytics, diagnostics, advertising and billing as described above. Content you explicitly share is delivered to the apps or recipients you choose.

8. Children's Privacy

Age Calculator is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at diyappstudio@gmail.com so we can address it.
