DIY AdMob Reports | DIY Apps Studio

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

This policy explains how DIY Apps Studio handles Google account authorisation and AdMob report data through its private reporting tool.

Permission and information processed

The tool requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admob.readonly. This allows read-only access to the connected publisher account. Report filters limit collection to configured apps, initially Age Calculator on Android. We do not receive the operator's Google password.

We process renewable authorisation, publisher and app identifiers, reporting timezone and currency, aggregate estimated earnings, requests, matched requests, impressions and clicks. Reports may include dates, ad units and their names, ad sources, ad formats, countries and app versions. We also record collection times, row counts, run status and error details. These reports do not include individual app users' advertising identifiers or browsing activity.

Use and transfer

We use this information for studio reporting, app improvement and investigation of reporting problems. We do not sell it, use it for personalised advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI or machine-learning models.

DIY AdMob Reports' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements where applicable.

Storage and access

Authorisation is stored in Google Cloud Secret Manager. The reporting pipeline stores imported raw reports in Google Cloud Storage and reporting tables in Google BigQuery. Authorisation values are excluded from public pages and source repositories. Google provides source reports and cloud processing services. dbt Cloud is used for reporting transformations. Authorised studio operators and service identities access information as needed for reporting and maintenance, under Google Cloud access controls. Providers may process information outside the operator's country.

Retention and deletion

Reports are retained for historical analysis and troubleshooting. There is no fixed automatic deletion period for all reports. To request removal of stored authorisation or imported reports, contact diyappstudio@gmail.com. Identify the relevant account or reports but do not send passwords or tokens. We confirm the requester's authority and review any legal or operational retention requirements.

Removing access

Remove DIY AdMob Reports from Google account connections to stop future collection after existing access tokens expire. Previously imported reports remain until separately deleted.

Security and website

We use encrypted connections and Google Cloud permissions to protect authorisation and report data. No system can guarantee complete security. These information pages have no connection form or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages may process connection information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes and contact

Updates will appear here with a revised effective date. Operator: DIY Apps Studio. Tool: DIY AdMob Reports. Support and privacy: diyappstudio@gmail.com.
